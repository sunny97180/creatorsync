FAQ

Questions, answered

How does the teleprompter follow my voice?
On-device speech recognition transcribes what you say and fuzzy-matches it against your script. The line only advances when you actually speak the next words — so pauses, ad-libs and emphasis never throw it off.
Can it really record two aspect ratios at once?
Yes. From a single performance CreatorSync writes both a 16:9 landscape file and a 9:16 portrait file, each framed around your face — no re-shooting or re-cropping later.
Which devices are supported?
CreatorSync is a universal app for macOS, iPhone and iPad. Your scripts and settings stay consistent across devices.
Is my data sent to the cloud?
No. Speech recognition and face tracking run entirely on-device, and recordings are saved locally. Nothing leaves your machine unless you choose to share it.
Do I need a special prompter rig?
Not at all — it works great on your screen as-is. If you use a beam-splitter rig, just enable Mirror mode and you're set.
