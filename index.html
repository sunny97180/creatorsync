How lip sync works

Three steps.
No reshoot.

Drop in the footage, drop in the audio, collect the render. The hard part — phoneme alignment, identity preservation, temporal smoothing — is ours.

  1. 01

    Upload your video

    Any framing, any resolution. We find and track the face, frame by frame.

  2. 02

    Add the audio

    A recording, a TTS render, a cloned voice. New language, new take — your call.

  3. 03

    Get the synced cut

    Same face, same lighting, new mouth. Delivered as a file or straight through the API.
